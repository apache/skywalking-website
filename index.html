AI FOR OBSERVABILITY

Bring AI to every investigation.

Explore your cloud-native and AI telemetry through Horizon's assistant or the AI client you already use.

IN HORIZON

Ask. Explore. Understand.

Ask about system health and performance. Get answers with charts, topology, traces, and logs, using the model you configure.

Use an OpenAI-compatible endpoint or Amazon Bedrock. Investigations follow your access permissions.

Explore the assistant →
IN YOUR AI CLIENT

Bring your own workflow.

Connect over MCP to use the same investigation tools and observability data from your desktop or terminal.

MCP Apps clients can render Horizon views. Terminal clients receive structured evidence to investigate.
